08 — How We Built This

Rigorous data. Trustworthy benchmarks.

Amplitude's behavioral database tracks billions of users across thousands of digital products. We analyzed and anonymized performance and engagement data across industries, regions, and company sizes. Data spanned September 2023 to September 2024.
